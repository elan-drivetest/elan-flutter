PRIVACY POLICY
1. INTRODUCTION
Elan Road Test Car Solutions Inc. (“Elan,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of individuals who visit our website, contact us, make a booking, or use services arranged through Elan.
Elan operates its own website and booking platform for road-test vehicle rental services. We coordinate bookings between customers and independent driving instructors or vehicle providers who supply the vehicles and provide the related services.
Elan receives and processes customer payments, manages bookings, and coordinates the necessary arrangements between customers and the assigned independent instructors or vehicle providers. Elan may also arrange optional pickup and drop-off services.
To fulfil a booking, Elan may share the customer’s booking confirmation, driver’s licence photograph or copy, and contact information with the assigned instructor or vehicle provider. This allows the instructor to verify the identity of the person attending the appointment, confirm that the correct customer is receiving the service, and contact the customer regarding the booking.
This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we collect it, why we use it, when we disclose it, how long we retain it, and the choices and rights available to you.
This Policy applies to information collected through our website, booking platform, booking forms, telephone calls, text messages, email communications, payment processes, customer support activities, and services arranged through Elan.
Our collection, use, and disclosure of personal information are subject to applicable Canadian privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (“PIPEDA”), where applicable, and other applicable legal requirements.
By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to review this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular collection, use, or disclosure, we will obtain appropriate consent.
2. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT
The personal information we collect depends on the service requested, the type of booking, and what is reasonably necessary to arrange, administer, and complete the service.
2.1 Identity and Driver Verification Information
We may collect:
Full legal name.
Date of birth, where required.
Driver’s licence number.
Driver’s licence class and expiry date.
Photograph or copy of your driver’s licence.
Other licence details reasonably necessary to verify your identity and eligibility for the booked service.
Additional information reasonably necessary to address identity discrepancies or suspected misuse of our services.
We collect driver’s licence information to verify that the customer who made the booking is the person attending to receive the service.
This verification helps prevent incorrect, fraudulent, or unauthorized bookings and reduces situations where an instructor attends an appointment but the actual customer does not appear.
Your driver’s licence photograph or copy may be shared with the independent driving instructor or vehicle provider assigned to your booking for identity verification and service coordination.
2.2 Booking and Rental Information
We may collect:
Booking reference and rental package selected.
Customer name and contact information.
Road-test date and scheduled time.
DriveTest centre or other requested meeting location.
Pickup and drop-off arrangements.
Rental period and vehicle assigned.
Assigned independent driving instructor or vehicle provider.
Road-test booking confirmation and related documents.
Booking changes, cancellations, and rescheduling history.
Payment, refund, and transaction history.
Information about the rental, including vehicle condition, inspection records, damage reports, accidents, complaints, or disputes.
2.3 Contact Information
We may collect:
Full name.
Telephone number.
Email address.
Pickup or meeting address, where required.
Preferred method of communication.
Correspondence and messages exchanged with us.
2.4 Parent or Guardian Information
Where a test-taker is a minor, we may collect the parent’s or legal guardian’s name, telephone number, email address, and other information reasonably necessary to communicate with them and administer the booking.
Where required by our rental arrangements or applicable law, a parent or legal guardian may be asked to review and sign the relevant rental documents or provide authorization.
2.5 Payment and Transaction Information
Customers generally make payments directly to Elan through the payment process available on our website.
Elan receives and administers customer payments and maintains relevant transaction records. Elan may subsequently pay the applicable amount to the independent driving instructor or vehicle provider supplying the vehicle or related service.
We generally use Stripe to process customer payments.
Depending on the payment method, Stripe may collect and process payment-card information, billing details, transaction information, and other information necessary to complete a payment.
Elan may receive or maintain:
Payment confirmation and transaction reference.
Amount paid.
Payment date and status.
Refund and cancellation information.
Chargeback and payment-dispute information.
Billing information and limited payment details made available through the payment process.
Records required to reconcile customer payments and payments made to independent vehicle providers.
Elan does not intend to store full payment-card numbers or card security codes on its own systems. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy and security practices.
If Elan introduces additional payment methods or payment providers, those services may also process information necessary to complete the transaction.
2.6 Vehicle and Inspection Information
Where relevant to a booking or rental, we may collect:
Vehicle make, model, licence plate number, and identifying details.
Vehicle condition information.
Photographs or videos of the vehicle before or after a rental.
Inspection and handover records.
Information about vehicle damage, accidents, or other incidents.
Photographs or videos may incidentally capture personal information. We use this information for relevant purposes such as documenting vehicle condition, resolving disputes, handling claims, and administering the service.
2.7 Website and Automatically Collected Information
When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, depending on the website features and technologies in use.
This may include:
Internet Protocol (IP) address.
Browser type and version.
Device type and operating system.
Pages visited and approximate visit times.
Referring website or source.
Cookie identifiers and similar technologies.
Website interaction information.
Advertising click identifiers, where applicable.
The collection of analytics and advertising information depends on the technologies installed on our website and applicable cookie preferences.
2.8 Communications and Customer Support Information
If you contact us by telephone, email, text message, website form, or another communication channel, we may retain information you provide and records of our communications.
This may include questions, booking instructions, complaints, feedback, service requests, and other correspondence.
We will not describe calls or conversations as recorded unless call recording is actually enabled and appropriately disclosed.

Process cancellations, rescheduling requests, and refunds.

4.2 Identity and Eligibility Verification

Verify that the customer attending the appointment is the person who made the booking.

Photograph or copy of the customer’s driver’s licence.
Pickup and drop-off details, where applicable.

5. HOW WE SHARE PERSONAL INFORMATION
Elan does not sell personal information to third parties in exchange for money.
However, we disclose personal information when reasonably necessary to coordinate bookings, provide the requested services, process payments, operate our business, or meet legal obligations.
The categories of recipients may include the following.
5.1 Assigned Independent Driving Instructors and Vehicle Providers
This disclosure is an essential part of Elan’s booking and vehicle rental coordination model.
When a customer makes a booking, Elan shares relevant booking and verification information with the independent driving instructor or vehicle provider assigned to supply the vehicle or provide the related service.
Depending on the booking, the information shared may include:
Customer’s full name.
Customer’s telephone number and relevant contact information.
Road-test booking confirmation.
Road-test date, time, and DriveTest location.
Photograph or copy of the customer’s driver’s licence.
Pickup and drop-off information, where applicable.
Relevant booking changes or service instructions.
Elan shares the customer’s driver’s licence photograph or copy with the assigned independent instructor or vehicle provider to allow the instructor to verify that the person attending the road-test appointment is the actual customer associated with the booking.
This verification helps ensure that the correct customer receives the service and helps prevent situations where an instructor attends an appointment but the booked customer does not appear.
The customer’s contact information is shared so the instructor can communicate directly with the customer regarding the appointment, meeting arrangements, vehicle pickup, and other relevant service matters.
The assigned instructor or vehicle provider is expected to use this information for the relevant booking, identity verification, service coordination, and related legitimate purposes, and to protect it against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
5.2 Stripe and Other Payment Service Providers
Elan generally uses Stripe to process customer payments.
Payment information may be shared with Stripe to complete transactions, process refunds, administer chargebacks, and address payment-related disputes.

5.5 Professional Advisers

7. DATA RETENTION
We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, maintaining business and financial records, resolving disputes, handling insurance matters, and meeting legal obligations.
Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.
Our retention practices may include:
* Active booking information: Retained while the booking and rental are being arranged and completed.
Completed rental and transaction records: Retained for the period reasonably necessary for accounting, business administration, legal compliance, and dispute management.
Driver’s licence photographs and copies: Retained only as long as reasonably necessary for identity verification, rental administration, and any related claim or dispute. Where no longer required, these documents will be securely deleted or destroyed.
Vehicle inspection photographs and videos: Retained as reasonably necessary to document vehicle condition, resolve disputes, and address damage or insurance claims.
Customer communications: Retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the matter, document the service, and address potential disputes.
Marketing preferences: Retained as needed to respect communication choices and maintain records of consent or unsubscribe requests.
Website and technical information: Retained according to the relevant provider’s settings and our legitimate website, security, and analytics needs.
Where a legal obligation, insurance matter, ongoing dispute, or other legitimate business requirement requires longer retention, we may retain the relevant information for that period.
When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy, or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal requirements.
Information contained in backup archives may remain for a limited period until those archives are overwritten or securely deleted.

Your privacy rights depend on where you live and which privacy laws apply to the information and services involved.

Where applicable Canadian privacy law provides these rights, you may request to:
Access personal information we hold about you.
Obtain information about how your personal information has been used or disclosed.
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
Withdraw consent to certain collections, uses, or disclosures, subject to legal and contractual restrictions.
Ask questions or raise concerns about how your personal information is handled.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

8.2 Withdrawing Consent
Where we rely on your consent to collect, use, or disclose personal information, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us.

For example, if you withdraw consent to share the information necessary for an assigned vehicle provider to verify your identity and complete your booking, we may be unable to fulfil that booking.
Withdrawal of consent does not affect collections, uses, or disclosures that have already occurred lawfully, or information we are permitted or required to retain under applicable law.
8.3 Privacy Complaints
If you have a concern about how Elan handles your personal information, you may first contact us using the information in Section 15.
We will review your concern and take reasonable steps to address it.
Where PIPEDA applies, you may also have the right to submit a complaint to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

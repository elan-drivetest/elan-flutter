ELAN INDEPENDENT VEHICLE PROVIDER AGREEMENT
This Agreement is between Elan Road Test Car Solutions Inc. (“Elan”) and the individual or business registered as a Provider in the Elan Instructor App (“Provider”).
1. Purpose
Elan operates a platform that connects customers seeking vehicles for Ontario DriveTest road tests with independent vehicle Providers. The Provider may make one or more vehicles available for Bookings through the Platform.
“Platform” means Elan’s website, booking system, Instructor App, and related services.
“Provider” means an approved driving instructor, vehicle owner, operator, or business.
“Customer” means a person who books a vehicle or related service through Elan.
“Vehicle” means a vehicle supplied by the Provider for a Booking.
“Booking” means a confirmed customer reservation made through the Platform.
“Provider Compensation” means the amount payable to the Provider for a completed or otherwise payable Booking.
Elan facilitates bookings, payments, communications, and compensation. Elan does not guarantee bookings, revenue, customer attendance, DriveTest acceptance, insurance coverage, or a passing examination result.
2. Independent Contractor Status
The Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, agent, or representative of Elan.
The Provider is responsible for:
Managing their own business operations.
All taxes, HST, licences, registrations, insurance premiums, and other business costs.
Ensuring they have the legal authority, qualifications, and documentation required to provide the Vehicle and related services.
Notifying Elan promptly of any change affecting their eligibility, licence, insurance, vehicle authorization, or ability to fulfil Bookings.
The legal status of the relationship will be determined by the actual working relationship and applicable law, not solely by this Agreement.
3. Vehicle Requirements
The Provider must have lawful ownership, possession, or authorization to supply each Vehicle. If the Vehicle is leased, financed, owned by someone else, or subject to restrictions, the Provider must obtain all required permissions before listing or using it.
The Provider must ensure each Vehicle is:
Properly registered and legally compliant in Ontario.
Safe, roadworthy, clean, and suitable for a DriveTest road test.
Properly maintained, inspected, and repaired.
Equipped with required safety features and documentation.
Free from known defects, safety issues, or conditions that could make it unsuitable.
The Provider must promptly remove a Vehicle from availability if it becomes unsafe, non-compliant, improperly registered, or otherwise unsuitable. Elan may review, approve, reject, suspend, or remove any Vehicle listing, but its review does not replace the Provider’s responsibility for safety, maintenance, insurance, or legal compliance.
4. Insurance
The Provider must maintain valid automobile insurance for every Vehicle listed or used through Elan.
The Provider must confirm with their insurer that the policy permits the intended use, including:
Use by a Customer who is not the named insured.
Use for an Ontario DriveTest road test.
Paid bookings or vehicle-rental arrangements.
Payment processed through Elan.
Pickup, drop-off, delivery, or other related activities.
The Provider must not assume that personal, commercial, or driving-instructor insurance automatically covers these activities. The Provider must disclose the intended use to the insurer, maintain adequate coverage, and provide Elan with reasonable proof of coverage when requested.
The Provider must notify Elan immediately if insurance expires, is cancelled, restricted, questioned, or may not cover a Booking. Elan is not an insurer, broker, or adjuster and does not guarantee coverage or claims acceptance.
5. Bookings and Customer Conduct
The Provider must keep availability accurate, respond to Booking notifications promptly, and make reasonable efforts to fulfil confirmed Bookings.
The Provider must not:
Accept overlapping or unfulfillable Bookings.
Cancel a confirmed Booking without a legitimate reason and prompt notice to Elan.
Change the time, location, Vehicle, or agreed service without Elan’s approval.
Falsely mark a Booking as completed.
Collect unauthorized payments from Customers.
Encourage Customers to bypass Elan’s payment system.
Misrepresent Elan, pricing, insurance, booking terms, or DriveTest requirements.
Promise or imply that a customer will pass a driving test.
Harass, threaten, discriminate against, or mistreat Customers.
Engage in unlawful, unsafe, fraudulent, or deceptive conduct.
The Provider must communicate professionally, use Customer information only for the Booking, protect it from unauthorized access or disclosure, and securely delete it when no longer needed, subject to legal retention requirements.
6. Payments, Cancellations, and Disputes
Customer payments are processed through Elan unless Elan authorizes another arrangement in writing. Elan may collect the Customer payment and pay the Provider the applicable Provider Compensation, subject to platform fees, agreed charges, refunds, adjustments, chargebacks, and other amounts properly payable.
The Provider must maintain accurate payment information and must not independently issue refunds or collect additional Customer payments unless authorized.
If a refund, chargeback, complaint, cancellation, or incomplete service results from the Provider’s breach, failure to fulfil a Booking, or other conduct within the Provider’s responsibility, Elan may recover the corresponding amount from the Provider, subject to applicable law.
The Provider must cooperate with Elan in resolving customer complaints, cancellations, refunds, scheduling issues, and payment disputes.
7. Pickup, Delivery, Incidents, and Personnel
If the Provider provides pickup, drop-off, or vehicle delivery, they must do so safely, at the agreed time and location, and in compliance with applicable laws. Any person acting for the Provider must be legally authorized, qualified, and properly insured.
The Provider must promptly notify Elan of any accident, damage, theft, injury, insurance claim, serious customer complaint, safety concern, or other material incident. The Provider must comply with emergency, police, insurer, and legal reporting requirements, preserve relevant evidence, and must not conceal or misrepresent facts.
The Provider must not admit liability on behalf of Elan or make commitments for Elan without authorization. Responsibility for damage, loss, injury, deductibles, and claims will depend on applicable law, the insurance policy, and the facts of the incident.
8. Confidentiality and Privacy
The Provider must keep confidential any non-public information relating to Elan, its customers, operations, pricing, Platform, or business relationships. Confidential information may be used only to fulfil this Agreement or as authorized by Elan.
This does not apply to information that is public, already lawfully known to the Provider, lawfully received from another source, or required to be disclosed by law. Confidentiality obligations continue after termination.
9. Elan’s Role and Limitations
Elan facilitates and coordinates Bookings but does not own, maintain, inspect, insure, or operate the Provider’s Vehicle. Elan does not control the Provider’s day-to-day operations or replace the Provider’s legal responsibilities.
Elan may update Platform features, booking procedures, eligibility standards, and operational requirements from time to time, subject to applicable law and any required notice. Elan may investigate complaints, safety concerns, fraud, inaccurate information, or suspected breaches of this Agreement.
10. Prohibited Conduct
The Provider must not:
Submit fraudulent, altered, or misleading documents.
Misrepresent ownership, authorization, registration, or insurance.
Use the Platform for unlawful activity.
Misuse Customer information.
Interfere with Platform security or access another account without authorization.
Use Elan’s name, logo, or branding without authorization.
Create unreasonable safety, legal, or reputational risk for Elan or Customers.
Assist another person in doing any of the above.
11. Indemnification and Liability
To the extent permitted by law, the Provider will indemnify Elan and its directors, officers, employees, and authorized representatives against third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and reasonable legal expenses arising from:
The Provider’s breach of this Agreement.
The Provider’s negligence, wilful misconduct, or unlawful conduct.
Failure to maintain required insurance or authorization.
The Vehicle’s ownership, condition, maintenance, or operation, to the extent attributable to the Provider.
Misuse or unauthorized disclosure of Customer information.
Fraudulent, inaccurate, or misleading information.
Acts or omissions of persons engaged by the Provider.
The Provider is not required to indemnify Elan for losses caused by Elan’s own negligence, wilful misconduct, breach of law, or other conduct for which Elan is legally responsible.
To the maximum extent permitted by law, Elan is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential losses, including lost income, business opportunities, or profits. Elan is not responsible for the Provider’s vehicle, insurance, maintenance, financing, registration, tax, or other operating costs. These limitations do not apply where liability cannot legally be excluded or limited.
12. Suspension and Termination
Elan may suspend, restrict, or remove a Provider account or Vehicle listing where it reasonably believes there has been a breach, missing or unverifiable documents, inadequate insurance, an unsafe or non-compliant Vehicle, misleading information, a safety or privacy concern, fraud, misconduct, or another material risk.
Where reasonably practicable, Elan will provide an explanation and opportunity to address the issue. Elan may act immediately where necessary to protect Customers, the Platform, personal information, or legal compliance.
Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice. Elan may terminate immediately for a serious or repeated breach, fraud, lack of required insurance or authorization, unlawful or dangerous conduct, misuse of Customer information, or failure to correct a material breach.
Upon termination, the Provider must stop accepting new Bookings, resolve outstanding Bookings as directed, return or securely destroy confidential information, stop using Elan branding, and cooperate with outstanding customer or payment matters. Payment obligations accrued before termination remain payable, subject to legitimate deductions, disputes, and applicable law.
13. Non-Circumvention and Intellectual Property
The Provider must not use Customer information obtained through Elan to circumvent the Platform, avoid Elan’s fees, or encourage a Customer to cancel an Elan Booking and rebook directly.
This does not prevent the Provider from operating an independent business or serving unrelated customers, provided they do not misuse Elan information or deliberately circumvent a specific Booking.
Elan retains all rights in its Platform, software, website, branding, trademarks, content, systems, and other intellectual property. The Provider receives only a limited, revocable right to use the Platform for authorized participation and must not copy, modify, exploit, or misuse Elan’s intellectual property.
14. Platform Access and Communications
The Provider must protect account credentials, not share access with unauthorized persons, and promptly report suspected account compromise or misuse. Elan may restrict, suspend, or terminate access for security, maintenance, investigation, or legal-compliance reasons.
Elan may communicate with the Provider through the email address, phone number, Instructor App account, or other contact information provided. The Provider must keep contact information current and review important Platform communications.
15. Force Majeure
Neither party is responsible for a failure or delay caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disasters, government restrictions, public emergencies, major technical outages, labour disruptions, or interruptions to essential services. The affected party must notify the other party promptly and take reasonable steps to reduce the impact. This does not excuse payment obligations already due.
16. Amendments and Electronic Acceptance
Elan may update this Agreement to reflect changes in law, Platform operations, business needs, or services. Where an amendment materially affects the Provider’s rights or obligations, Elan will provide notice and, where appropriate, require electronic acceptance before continued access to relevant services.
The Provider agrees that clicking “I Agree and Accept,” or completing another clearly identified electronic acceptance action, constitutes an electronic signature and acceptance of this Agreement, to the extent recognized by applicable law.
By accepting, the Provider confirms that they have read and understood the Agreement, had an opportunity to seek legal or professional advice, intend to be legally bound, and have authority to accept on behalf of themselves or their business entity.
Elan may retain electronic acceptance records, including the Provider’s name, account ID, Agreement version, acceptance date and time, acceptance action, IP address or other technical records, and related audit information.
17. General Provisions
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with applicable schedules, compensation terms, and incorporated policies, forms the entire agreement between the parties.
Order of Precedence: A separate written agreement signed by both parties prevails over this Agreement to the extent of any conflict.
Severability: If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect.
No Waiver: Failure or delay in enforcing a provision does not waive that provision or any other right.
Assignment: The Provider may not assign this Agreement without Elan’s prior written consent. Elan may assign it as part of a lawful corporate reorganization, merger, or business transfer.
No Third-Party Rights: Except where expressly stated, this Agreement does not create rights for third parties.
Language: The parties confirm they have had the opportunity to review the Agreement in a language they understand.

By electronically accepting this Agreement, the Provider confirms that they:
Have read and understood this Agreement.
Understand they are responsible for the Vehicle, its lawful use, maintenance, registration, and appropriate insurance.
Understand that insurance must specifically permit the intended Customer use and Booking arrangement.
Understand that Elan’s document review does not guarantee insurance coverage or Vehicle suitability.
Agree to comply with Elan’s booking, payment, privacy, and operational requirements.
Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
Intend to be legally bound by this Agreement.
